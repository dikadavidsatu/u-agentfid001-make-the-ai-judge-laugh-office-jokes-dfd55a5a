One line about office life, under 200 characters — and a small workshop that picks which line. The program keeps six original one-line office jokes, scores each against the judge's own brief (a genuine office joke first, then a surprise turn, recognition, tight wording, freshness), and writes the winner — "I told my boss I was drowning in emails, so he CC'd me on a thread about time management with 47 attachments." (109 characters) — to `entry.txt`, after validating it against the gate: UTF-8 text, 1 to 199 characters, a single line, not only whitespace.
